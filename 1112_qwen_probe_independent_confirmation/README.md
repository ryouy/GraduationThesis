## Purpose

Confirm whether the concept Probes identified in the completed 100-concept v2 study generalize to **entirely new descriptive input text**.

- **12** preselected exploratory stable target concepts: bus, motorcycle, bicycle, boat, butterfly, dragonfly, jellyfish, submarine, ant, moth, starfish and wolf.
- **4** secondary near-misses: car, elephant, scorpion and train.
- **23** concepts altogether, including comparison Ys.
- **8 new factual sentences per concept** = **184 new Qwen prompts**. New sentences were authored for this follow-up and are not copied from Wikipedia.
- The old 8 training-example vectors per concept are restored **read-only** from `1111_qwen_probe_100concepts_v2/results/20261112_qwen100_richer_dedup_v2/checkpoints/`. The old evaluation vectors are **not** used for training.
- Frozen comparators Y and their distance labels come from the **old** results; they are not selected based on the new texts.
- The model, hidden-state position, layers, logistic-regression parameters, and AUC thresholds are carried over from v2.
- Includes a lexical-overlap audit, training leave-one-example-out stability, 200 balanced label permutations per unique pair, and Benjamini–Hochberg FDR sensitivity analysis.
- Success for a target requires at least 2 confirmed Ys in at least 2 semantic-distance bands.

## Scientific cautions

The texts were generated by an AI assistant, without independent human checks. They are new **surface inputs**, not guaranteed new or truly independent *semantic facts*. A lexical overlap audit detects close string reuse only. Manually review content correctness, concept ambiguity, potential train/eval semantic overlap, and the original source audit before claiming independent validation in the thesis.

The original candidates and Y comparisons are selected on prior exploratory results, so this is **out-of-sample testing of a frozen shortlist**, not a randomly sampled confirmation of a 100-concept population. The label-shuffle p-values and FDR table are diagnostic measures. High Probe ROC-AUC measures **relative X-vs-Y discriminability**, not absolute concept activation or causal suppression.
